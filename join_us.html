As Postdocs

1. JSPS Postdoctoral Fellowships for Research in Japan (PD 特別研究員/外国人特別研究員)
JSPS offers several types of postdoctoral fellowships for researchers to conduct research in Japan.
• For International Fellowships, the duration is up to 2 years, and application deadlines are typically in April and August each year. Details can be found here.
• For Domestic Fellowships (JSPS PD), intended for researchers based in Japan, the duration is up to 3 years, with applications usually due in early June each year. Detials can be found here.

2. Postdoc supported by Project Budget
We also welcome postdoctoral researchers funded by our research projects. If you are interested in this option, please contact Prof. Guo directly to inquire about available projects and funding conditions.
